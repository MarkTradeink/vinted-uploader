# Photo guide

For each item, take a complete front view, a back view, close-ups of brand/size and
composition labels, and close-ups of defects or important details. Use natural
indirect light and a plain background. Photograph included accessories separately.

Keep photos of the same item together where convenient, or use one subfolder per
item and `prepare --recursive`. Grouping still needs visual review. A pause between
shots, similar filenames, or similar colors does not establish item identity.

Measure chest width and garment length for tops; flat waist and inseam for trousers.
Record centimeters and how you measured. Do not estimate dimensions from a hanger.

Keep original files. Contact sheets are inspection aids and do not replace originals.
The GIF decoder previews its first frame. HEIC requires the optional decoder.
An unreadable file must be resolved or explicitly excluded, never silently dropped.
